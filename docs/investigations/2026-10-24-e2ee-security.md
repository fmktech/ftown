Attack goals are plaintext bypass, sender/key substitution, cross-owner/bridge operations, read-to-control/admin escalation, replay after restart/revocation, secret/content leakage and loss of mandatory audit evidence. Boundaries are cloud/local/direct input to HPKE verification, verified principals to business dispatch, filesystem policy/key persistence, and trusted hook IPC versus arbitrary application access. OS compromise and malicious same-origin browser code are outside this protocol's claim.
