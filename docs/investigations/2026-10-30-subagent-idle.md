## Frozen product intent (review approved)

Repeated Stop does not reset deadline. Busy, session end, stop/removal, or shutdown cancels before enqueue. Unknown→idle stays conservative (no inferred historical work). Questions and explicit human attention stay unchanged. Remove human idle producer and suppress authenticated legacy idle push events server-side.
